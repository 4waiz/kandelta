---
name: Visual relevance boundaries
description: Why visual matches and reviewed examples must remain separate from population and local product claims
---

Apply a conservative second-stage topic review only to sampled example posts, creator candidates, and creative references. Keep Oriane's matched-population counts, rates, and denominators intact; do not extrapolate the reviewed sample's pass rate to the full result set. An on-topic caption or transcript still does not establish a product test, actual temperature, or local-market demand.

**Why:** The verified Running shoes UAE snapshot contained high-scoring visual matches for unrelated entertainment and figurative uses of running. Its measured universe also fell back to global running content when the local slice was too thin, so apparent relevance to running is not proof of UAE product performance.

**How to apply:** In future angle, brief, or presentation changes, label raw visual metrics as Oriane-filtered population statistics, identify the separately qualified sample, and disclose the topic exclusion reasons. Require independent evidence before making geographic or product claims.
